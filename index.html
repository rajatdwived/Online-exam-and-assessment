Exam rules

Please read these points before you begin a test.

  1. Log in only with the enrollment ID issued by your college.
  2. Entry closes 10 minutes after the test has started.
  3. You can return to any question and change your answer.
  4. Switching tabs or windows is recorded as a warning.
  5. Answers stay saved even if your internet drops.
  6. The paper submits automatically when the timer ends.
